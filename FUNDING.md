# Supporting Sky Omega

Sky Omega is an independent, open-source research initiative released under the MIT License.

Financial contributions are welcome but are not a prerequisite for the project’s existence or continued development.

Contributions may support research infrastructure, computational resources, experimentation, engineering, documentation, and the dissemination of research results.

Financial support does not confer ownership, privileged access, or authority over the project’s scientific or technical direction.

Research independence and openness remain fundamental principles.

Funding is an enabler, not an objective.
